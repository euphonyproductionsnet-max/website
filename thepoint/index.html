About This Audiobook

Clandestine methamphetamine labs crept into Texas during the latter half of the 20th century, but certain members of the Texas Department of Public Safety's Narcotics Division are hellbent on stopping the malicious tide. Agents Dan Allen and his rookie partner, Kate Starnes, encounter a multi-layered criminal organization operating meth labs in the East Texas piney woods.

Dan, in an undercover capacity, infiltrates to the highest levels of the meth clan, while attempting to balance his home life and family responsibilities. The investigation pulls him into a realm of cash, corruption and malevolence. He struggles to partition his two lives, but the lines become distorted.

Texas Ranger John Wilson, Dan's former Highway Patrol partner, is called to investigate a double homicide within the same crime syndicate. Forensic evidence lead Ranger Wilson down troubling paths. The Ranger and the Narcotics Agent confront difficult decisions that will forever change their lives.
